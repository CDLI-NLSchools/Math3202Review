# Math 3202 Review

A generated practice question bank for **Mathematics 3202** (Applied stream, Newfoundland and Labrador), built on the same chassis as the Math 7, 8, 9, 1201, 2200, 2201, 3200, 3201, and 3208 Review apps, with the junior scaffolding set: staged hints, worked examples, and immediate feedback.

**Live app:** https://cdli-nlschools.github.io/Math3202Review/ (once GitHub Pages is enabled)

## What it does

- Opens on a simple student face: the selected scope, one line about what will happen, and one large start button. The full builder sits collapsed under "Build your own practice". If no units are chosen, the start button opens the builder and scrolls to it.
- Draws fresh practice questions by unit or by curriculum indicator. The bank holds 155 entries covering all 74 curriculum indicators across the ten units. Unit 9 also carries 5 prior-learning review entries on the primary trigonometric ratios (see Sources below), so the governed bank is 150 entries plus those 5.
- No mid-course or final exam presets: this course has none. Instead, each unit has a practice test modelled on the development shell's unit tests: 8 selected-response questions at 1 mark each, plus 2 constructed-response questions drawn across the unit's indicators, for per-unit totals of 15 to 18 marks (Unit 1: 15, Unit 2: 17, Unit 3: 15, Unit 4: 16, Unit 5: 17, Unit 6: 16, Unit 7: 17, Unit 8: 18, Unit 9: 15, Unit 10: 16). Practice tests are built one unit at a time; asking for one across several units returns guidance instead of a paper.
- Practice tests offer an optional soft timer modelled on a 1 hour sitting (a single setting, TEST_MINUTES, drives the label). It counts up, never locks or submits anything. Note: the development shell's own unit tests are set to 120 minutes; the 1 hour model is the project decision and is flagged as item 1 on the QA spot-check list.
- Three ways to work: practice online with check-as-you-go marking, print a practice sheet (with working space and an optional answer key), or print a unit practice test with a separate teacher key. Sheets and tests can also be downloaded as a Word file with native editable equations, or copied for Google Docs.
- Every practice question carries a staged hint (strategy, then first step), a "See a worked example" button that shows the same skill with different numbers, per-answer feedback on multiple choice questions, and a full worked solution. The worked example and the worked solution are show/hide toggles: hiding and re-showing never changes what was dealt, and a separate "Show another example" button inside the example panel is the only way to deal a fresh example (in seeded sets the sequence of examples is reproducible, press by press). The end-of-set summary lists any indicators missed and offers a "Practice what I missed" set. A collapsible reference panel sits at the top of each practice set; Unit 7's panel carries the official formula sheet's polygon formulas and Unit 9's carries the sine and cosine laws in both forms, exactly as printed on the Math 3202 Formula Sheet (2025-2026).
- Teachers can set a seed word or phrase in the builder ("Same problems for everyone"): a shared link carrying that seed gives every student the identical set, same questions, same values, same order, including the worked examples. With no seed, each student gets a fresh random set.
- Any online practice set can be downloaded for Kahoot (.xlsx, in Kahoot's template layout) or Blooket (.csv, in Blooket's template layout) to run the same multiple choice questions as a game, with the math written as readable plain text and a time limit set by question level (20, 30 or 60 seconds). The downloads live on the online practice view only; practice sheets and practice tests are show-your-work print products and do not offer them. Constructed-response questions are not exported, and the app says how many were left out.
- Contexts follow the applied stream: workplace and everyday situations in the Math at Work style, plain short stems, and money amounts realistic for Newfoundland and Labrador (HST at 15% where tax applies, with rates stated in the stem where a question depends on them).

## Sources and originality

Items are generated from templates written for this bank. Unit placement and indicator text are governed by the official Applied Mathematics 3202 units and indicators spreadsheet (74 unique indicators, codes MA3202.<target>.<indicator>; the app shows the number without the MA3202 prefix, following the junior apps' convention). The public curriculum viewer's indicator listings (75) were reconciled against the spreadsheet: the one viewer-only listing, a Unit 5 formula indicator, has no spreadsheet counterpart and is not covered. The practice-test shape comes from the Mathematics 3202 development shell's unit tests, used as a structural model only. No question text, scenario, or values from the shell pools, the viewer, or any other source are reproduced, and the app does not mirror the actual unit tests item for item.

Unit 9 bridging set (Jason's design addition, November 7, 2026): because the 3202 prerequisite is Applied 1202 or Academic 1201, many students arrive without 2202, where the primary ratios get their fuller treatment. Five review entries (ids u9r-) refresh ratio values for an acute angle, finding a side, finding an angle, and an angle of elevation or depression context, feeding the sine and cosine law work. They are tagged in the working records to the 1202 indicators (MA1202.5.x) and are not counted as 3202 indicator coverage; the 5.1 to 5.4 coverage stands on the unit's 14 governed entries alone.

Levels: no table of specifications is published for this course. The bank's level mix (about 20% Level 1, 60% Level 2, 20% Level 3; actual whole-bank census 21.3 / 59.4 / 19.4) follows the 20/60/20 split used across the other courses as a design prior, not a specification.
